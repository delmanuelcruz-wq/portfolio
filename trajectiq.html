TrajectIQ™

An AI career-intelligence platform — built solo, end-to-end, with Anthropic's Claude Code.

01

The Problem

Job seekers are told to “tailor your resume and apply more” — generic advice that ignores where they actually stand. TrajectIQ delivers a personalized career-identity diagnosis, an honest read on the gaps holding a search back, and the next three specific moves — in under five minutes, free.

02

My Role

I built the platform end-to-end, solo, using Anthropic's Claude Code as AI development partner — from product taxonomy and revenue model to full-stack engineering, security, deployment, and public launch. No outside engineering team; zero infrastructure budget.

03

The Stack

Supabase · Auth · Postgres · RLSNetlify serverless functionsAnthropic Claude APIGoogle OAuth2PayPal subscriptionsGitHub Actions CI/CDVanilla-JS SPAGA4 · Open Graph
04

Engineering Highlights

  • Full authentication & security stack — Supabase email/password + passwordless magic-link login, Google OAuth2, Row-Level Security policies, and Content-Security-Policy headers.
  • IP protected by architecture — the proprietary scoring engine lives in Netlify serverless functions, so the core logic never reaches the browser.
  • Conversion-first funnel — replaced the account-creation wall with a frictionless one-field email lead-capture (works in any browser or in-app webview), instrumented with GA4 funnel tracking and Open Graph social-share optimization.
  • Launch-day monetization — a 3-tier freemium model with live PayPal subscription billing, serverless plan provisioning, and webhook-driven lifecycle sync.
